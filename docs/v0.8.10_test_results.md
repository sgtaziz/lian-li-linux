## 2. Troubleshooting Physical LCD: Image Retention & Flickering

### 2.1 Symptom Description
After displaying static images or high-contrast sensor overlays for prolonged periods:
1. **Ghosting / Image Retention:** Shadows or outlines of previous images remain visible regardless of media content.
2. **High-Frequency Flicker / Flashing:** Upon restoring brightness (e.g., to 75%), the retained ghost image appears to strobe or flash rapidly at 30–60 Hz.
3. **Apparent Persistence After 0% Brightness:** Leaving the display at `brightness: 0` during PC sleep/suspend does not automatically dissipate the retention.

### 2.2 Physics & Root Cause Analysis

> [!NOTE]
> **IPS LCD vs. OLED Degradation**  
> Unlike OLED displays, which suffer irreversible organic emissive material decay ("burn-in"), the HydroShift AIO uses a **thin-film transistor (TFT) IPS liquid crystal panel**. Liquid crystals do not burn out. Image persistence on IPS panels is caused by electrostatic charge accumulation and is **fully reversible**.

1. **Polarity Inversion Flicker:**
   - LCD driver ICs alternate voltage polarity across each pixel (positive $+V$ and negative $-V$) at 30 Hz / 60 Hz to prevent net direct-current (DC) bias.
   - When a high-contrast static image is displayed continuously, mobile ions inside the liquid crystal layer migrate and become trapped against the polyimide alignment layers, creating a parasitic internal DC offset.
   - When the AC driving voltage alternates, the trapped parasitic DC adds to the positive frame and subtracts from the negative frame:
     $$V_{\text{eff}}^{+} = V_{\text{drive}} + V_{\text{parasitic}}$$
     $$V_{\text{eff}}^{-} = -V_{\text{drive}} + V_{\text{parasitic}}$$
   - This creates an asymmetrical optical transmission between consecutive frames. The human eye perceives this luminance disparity as a **rapid 30–60 Hz flashing or strobing effect** localized to the ghosted areas.

2. **Why 0% Brightness During Sleep Did Not Clear Retention:**
   - Setting `brightness: 0` disables the **LED backlight**, but modern ATX motherboards supply continuous **+5V standby power (+5VSB)** to internal USB headers even when suspended or asleep.
   - Because the LCD driver IC and internal USB bus remained powered, the liquid crystals maintained their static polarization state instead of discharging.

### 2.3 Recovery Procedures

#### Procedure A: Active Dynamic Pixel Exerciser (In Progress)
The fastest method to collapse parasitic DC offset while the system is operating is driving full dynamic range AC voltage transitions across the entire matrix.

- **Clearing Asset:** [`~/.config/lianli/pixel_cleaner.mp4`](file:///home/gman/.config/lianli/pixel_cleaner.mp4) (480×480 H.264 loop)
  - **Phase 1 (10 Hz B/W Strobing):** Alternates pure black (`#000000`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to force rapid polarization inversions.
  - **Phase 2 (Dynamic Static / Noise):** High-contrast pseudo-random noise exercising individual sub-pixel transistors independently.
  - **Phase 3 (Primary Color Flush):** Solid Red, Green, Blue, and White cycles to normalize color filter transmission.
- **Operating Parameters:** 70%–75% brightness for **30 to 60 minutes**.

#### Procedure B: Cold Power-Drain (Zero-Potential Passive Discharge)
To achieve complete passive molecular relaxation without active video playback, all residual voltage must be purged from the motherboard and USB rail:

1. **Shut down the PC** cleanly via the operating system.
2. **Switch off the physical PSU toggle** (or disconnect the AC power cord).
3. **Depress and hold the PC chassis power button for 15–20 seconds.**
   *(This discharges the filter capacitors on the motherboard +5VSB rail and internal USB headers).*
4. Leave the system unpowered for **1 to 2 hours** (or overnight).
5. Upon cold boot, the liquid crystals will return to their relaxed, neutral state.

### 2.4 Preventative Operational Guidelines

To prevent recurrence of image retention on the HydroShift LCD:

- **Limit Continuous Maximum Brightness:** Maintain operating brightness between **60% and 75%**. High brightness increases heat within the pump housing, accelerating ion migration.
- **Avoid 24/7 Static Elements:** When displaying sensor data or images, use moving animations, video loops, or rotate gauge templates every few hours.
- **Configure Sleep States:** For prolonged idle periods, ensure the PC enters deep S5 (powered down) or enable complete USB power-off in BIOS (`ErP Ready` enabled in BIOS settings to cut 5VSB power when off).
